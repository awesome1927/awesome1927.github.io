ABOUT THIS PAGE

Skylanders was a series a video games that began in October of 2011 and the (currently) final game in the series releasing October of 2016. A primary feature of this series were the playable characters which required the use of a physical toy to use thanks to the use of nfc technology. This page shows only a small portion of the many characters released for the series.
